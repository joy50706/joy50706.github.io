I am **Dr. Jing Xu**, a Postdoctoral Researcher at the **South Australian immunoGENomics Cancer Institute (SAiGENCI), Adelaide University**. I received my Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Monash University, supervised by Prof. Jiangning Song, Prof. Jian Li and Dr. Chen Li. Before that, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology (2020) and B.S. in Automation (2017) from Nankai University, both supervised by Prof. Han Zhang.

My research lies at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, bioinformatics and computational biology**, with a focus on **peptide and protein modelling and design**. I develop machine-learning approaches to study biomolecular activity, structure–function relationships and molecular interactions. My work on bioactive peptides includes antimicrobial peptide prediction, generative design and terminal chemical modifications. {% if site.show_peptide_protein_project == true %}I am also developing approaches for **peptide–protein interaction prediction**, alongside research in microbiome-derived peptides, multi-omics and precision medicine.{% else %}My research also spans microbiome-derived peptides, multi-omics and precision medicine.{% endif %}

Building on my work in bioactive peptide discovery, I aim to extend my research to **protein modelling and design**, with an emphasis on sequence–structure–function relationships and molecular recognition. I am particularly interested in the **target-specific design of short peptide binders, cyclic and macrocyclic peptides, and peptides incorporating noncanonical amino acids**. By integrating protein language models, generative modelling and structure-guided design, I aim to explore how sequence, cyclization and chemical modifications can be jointly optimized for binding affinity, selectivity and proteolytic stability, as well as cellular permeability where required. In collaboration with experimental researchers, I hope to develop and evaluate candidates for modulating protein function and therapeutically relevant interactions.

**Research interests:** Protein Modelling and Design · Target-Specific Peptide Design · Cyclic and Macrocyclic Peptides · Noncanonical Amino Acids · Protein Language Models and Generative Modelling · Bioactive Peptide Discovery.
