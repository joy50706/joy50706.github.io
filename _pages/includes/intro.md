I am **Dr. Jing Xu**, a Postdoctoral Researcher at the **South Australian immunoGENomics Cancer Institute (SAiGENCI), Adelaide University**. I received my Ph.D. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology from Monash University, supervised by Prof. Jiangning Song, Prof. Jian Li and Dr. Chen Li. Before that, I earned my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology (2020) and B.S. in Automation (2017) from Nankai University, both supervised by Prof. Han Zhang.

My research lies at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, bioinformatics and computational biology**, with a focus on **peptide and protein modelling and design**. I develop machine-learning approaches to study biomolecular activity, structure–function relationships and molecular interactions. My work on bioactive peptides includes antimicrobial peptide prediction, generative design and terminal chemical modifications. I am also developing approaches for **peptide–protein interaction prediction**, alongside research in microbiome-derived peptides, multi-omics and precision medicine.

Building on this work, I aim to connect sequence-based learning with the physical behaviour of biomolecules. My future research interests include **integrating protein language models with physics-based simulation**, incorporating **protein dynamics and conformational ensembles** into design, and improving generative approaches for **complex molecular interfaces**, including protein–membrane and protein–sugar interactions. I am interested in combining generative modelling, structure prediction and inverse folding with molecular dynamics, working with experimental collaborators to evaluate predictions and guide further design.

**Research interests:** AI for Peptide and Protein Design · Peptide–Protein Interactions · Protein Language Models · Physics-Informed Generative Modelling · Protein Dynamics and Molecular Interfaces · Bioactive Peptide Discovery.
